How our care process works

Caring for someone is personal. Getting support should feel simple.

You may be looking for help for a parent, supporting a partner through recovery, or arranging care for a relative whose needs have changed. You may not even know which service to ask for yet. That is okay. We can start with a conversation.

What happens when you contact Obuntu?

Six clear steps, from your first message to the first day of care.

Book a consultation
  1. 1

    Contact the team

    Call, email or WhatsApp us. Tell us about the patient and the help needed.

  2. 2

    We discuss your needs

    Our team asks relevant questions to understand the type of care required, the location, expected schedule, and any clinical instructions.

  3. 3

    Physical assessment

    Our team visits the patient for a physical assessment of their needs.

  4. 4

    We clarify the options

    If the requested care is within our service scope, we explain the possible arrangement, availability, costs, and any next steps required.

  5. 5

    We agree the arrangement

    If both parties agree, we confirm the arrangement and explain how care will be coordinated. We then sign a contract.

  6. 6

    Care begins

    Your assigned nurse provides support in line with the care plan. Needs change, so we keep the patient, family and healthcare professionals in touch.
