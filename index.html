Why we do what we do

Love is there. Time is not always.

We all have people we would do anything for.

  • A parent
  • A grandparent
  • A spouse
  • A child

When they fall ill, our instinct is to be there — to sit beside them, make sure they are comfortable, and remind them that they are loved. But life doesn’t always make that easy. Work continues. Children need attention. Responsibilities pile up.

At Obuntu, we understand that gap.

We help families navigate it with compassionate nursing and bedside care that supports the person receiving care and the people who love them. Because being cared for should mean feeling seen, respected and supported.

How it works

Getting support should feel simple.

See the full process
1

Talk to us

Call, email or WhatsApp. Tell us about the patient and the help needed. You don’t need a complete plan.

2

We visit and assess

We learn about your needs, visit the patient in person, then explain the options, availability and costs.

3

Care begins

Once we agree the arrangement, your assigned nurse starts care — and we keep everyone in touch as needs change.

Questions families ask us.

The cost may depend on the service, level of nursing support, duration, schedule and location. Contact us for a quotation based on the arrangement you need.

You do not need to arrive with a complete care plan. Start by explaining your concerns and the support you think would help. We can discuss whether your needs fit our services and what the next step should be.

We can discuss post-operative and recovery support. The suitability of the arrangement depends on the discharge plan, the nursing needs and the services Obuntu can provide.

Home nursing arrangements should not be treated as a substitute for emergency care. If someone is experiencing a medical emergency, seek immediate assistance from the nearest appropriate emergency or health facility.
